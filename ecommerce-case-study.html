E-Commerce Commercial Performance & Sales Control Center

A multi-page commercial intelligence application built to support analysis of GMV, MRP Sales, G2N dilution leakage and Blended AOV, with FY25/FY26 year-over-year analysis and automated Excel reporting.

Python • Streamlit • Pandas • NumPy • OpenPyXL

Business Problem

The workflow required commercial metrics and year-over-year analysis to be consolidated from transactional data into clear, repeatable reporting.

  • Track core commercial KPIs.
  • Compare FY25 and FY26 performance.
  • Analyze G2N dilution leakage and Blended AOV.
  • Reduce repetitive manual reporting work.

Solution

Built a multi-page Streamlit application supported by reusable Pandas ETL pipelines and an automated Excel reporting engine.

  • Ingested, sanitized and aggregated transactional data.
  • Engineered multi-dimensional YoY analysis.
  • Generated structured Excel reports programmatically.
  • Designed outputs for practical commercial decision support.

Data & Reporting Pipeline

Designed the workflow from raw transactional inputs through analysis and reporting.

Transactional Data→Data Cleaning→Pandas ETL→KPI Aggregation→YoY Analysis→Streamlit / Excel

Key Commercial Metrics

GMV

Gross Merchandise Value monitoring and comparison.

MRP Sales

Monitoring of MRP-based sales performance.

G2N Dilution Leakage

Analysis of commercial leakage affecting net realization.

Blended AOV

Average order value analysis across dimensions.

Technology

PythonStreamlitPandasNumPyOpenPyXL

OpenPyXL was used to automate Excel reporting with multi-level headers, programmatic formatting and dynamic column sizing.

Professional Contribution

Contributed to the design and implementation of a practical analytics workflow connecting transactional data processing, commercial KPI analysis, interactive visualization and automated reporting.

The project demonstrates the ability to translate operational business requirements into repeatable data workflows and usable analytics outputs.
